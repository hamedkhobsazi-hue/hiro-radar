# Hiro Radar

**Hiro Radar** is HiHiro's AI-assisted business monitoring service for recurring public-source monitoring.

It is designed for teams and individuals who repeatedly need to watch public signals such as:

- buyer intent, service requests and lead opportunities;
- competitor pricing, launches and positioning changes;
- public RFPs, contracts, grants and procurement notices;
- market intelligence and category movement;
- meaningful website, price and availability changes.

Hiro Radar follows a controlled loop:

**Monitor → Detect → Filter → Verify → Deliver → Repeat**

## Boundaries

Hiro Radar does not claim or provide:

- authentication or CAPTCHA bypass;
- credential theft;
- rate-limit evasion;
- private-source access without authorization;
- mass unsolicited outreach;
- physical weather, aviation, maritime, military or vehicle radar.

Sensitive, regulated or high-impact use cases may require review or may be declined.
